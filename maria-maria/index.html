How to play it

Left hand. Am7 (x02010) is an open A minor with your 3rd finger lifted, so the open G string rings. For Dm7 (xx0211), lay your 1st finger flat across the B and high E strings at fret 1, and put your 2nd finger on the G string at fret 2. Your 2nd finger only moves from the D string to the G string, so keep it close to the strings.

Right hand. Classical-style fingerpicking: p (thumb) plays the bass, i plays the G string, and m + a pinch the B and high E strings together. Per bar: bass · i · m+a · i · bass · i · m+a · i. Keep it light and even, with a slight push on the pinches.

On the record. The whole song rides on this A minor loop. Carlos Santana's solos improvise over it, mostly in A minor pentatonic and A Dorian/Aeolian around the 5th and 12th frets. This page teaches the accompaniment loop, not a note-for-note copy of his solos.
